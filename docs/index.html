THE RULE OF "SPLIT & LEAP"

Unlike chess or checkers, your cows move in straight hex lines until hitting an obstacle or pasture edge.

1️⃣ Leave at least 1 cow behind to hold ground
2️⃣ Launch remaining herd in a straight line
3️⃣ Encircle rival herds so they cannot escape!
Fast & Intuitive

HOW TO PLAY IN 60 SECONDS

No complicated handbooks or tedious setup. Battle Cows takes 1 minute to understand and a lifetime of fun to master.

1
🧩

Build the Pasture

At the start of every match, each player takes turns placing procedural hex clusters. You create the battlefield together — no two maps are ever the same!

💡 Pro Tip: Create narrow bottlenecks to trap aggressive rivals early!
2
🐄

Stack Your Herd

Place your full 16-cow herd on any edge tile. Your fortress is ready — now plan your offensive vectors across the meadow.

💡 Pro Tip: Corners provide safety, but center positions offer maximum mobility.
3
⚡

Split & Sprint

Select your stack, choose how many cows stay behind, and launch the rest in a straight hex direction until stopped by fences or tile edges.

💡 Pro Tip: Never leave single cows isolated where they can get walled off!
4
👑

Dominate & Win

When all herds are trapped or the meadow is completely occupied, the player controlling the highest number of pasture tiles wins the game!

💡 Pro Tip: Control special Golden Pastures for massive territory score multipliers!

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Battle Cows, matchmaking, rules, and privacy.

How is Battle Cows related to the board game Battle Sheep? +
Battle Cows is heavily inspired by the beloved hex-territory mechanics popularized by the tabletop board game classic Battle Sheep (originally designed by Francesco Rotta and published by Blue Orange Games). If you love splitting stacks of animals, leaping across pasture hexes to block opponents, and conquering territory, Battle Cows brings that exact tactical thrill to mobile—enhanced with a 50+ stage solo campaign, smart AI, dynamic special tiles (like Golden Pastures, Fence Gates, and Rocket Pads), and convenient pass-and-play without needing to carry a physical box!
Is Battle Cows a good mobile alternative to Battle Sheep? +
Yes, it is the premier mobile alternative! Battle Cows preserves the pure 60-second learning curve and deep emergent tactics of Battle Sheep, while adding procedural board variety, offline single-player AI, puzzle challenges, and zero setup time.
Is Battle Cows really 100% playable offline? +
Yes! Battle Cows is fully functional without an active internet connection. All single-player campaign puzzles, Pass & Play tabletop multiplayer, and computer AI matches run entirely on your device. It is ideal for road trips, flights, and commutes.
How many players can play together on one device? +
Up to 4 players can battle on the same mobile phone or tablet using our Pass & Play mode. Each player gets their own vibrant herd color (Blue, Red, Yellow, Purple) and takes turns maneuvering on the procedural hex board.
Is the game appropriate for young children? +
Absolutely. Battle Cows contains zero violence, no aggressive timers, and features wholesome cartoon bovine graphics. It is a fantastic spatial thinking and logic game for kids and families.
Which languages are supported? +
Battle Cows includes native multilingual support in English, French (Français), and Arabic (العربية) with complete Right-to-Left (RTL) interface alignment and custom fonts.
